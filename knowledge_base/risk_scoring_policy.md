# Vendor Risk Scoring Policy

The Risk Review process evaluates vendors using several business risk factors.

Examples of risk indicators include:

- Foreign vendor
- Missing Certificate of Insurance
- Data-sensitive vendor
- IT/Software vendor
- New vendor with no prior relationship
- Vendor appearing on a sanctions or watchlist

Each risk indicator contributes to the vendor's overall risk score.

General guidance:

Low Risk:
Routine onboarding may continue.

Medium Risk:
Additional reviewer validation is recommended.

High Risk:
The request must be escalated for human review before approval.

Risk scores are recommendations and do not replace reviewer judgment.
