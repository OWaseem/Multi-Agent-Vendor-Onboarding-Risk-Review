# Required Document Checklist

Every vendor must provide the following documents before onboarding can
continue:

- IRS W-9 (or equivalent tax documentation)
- Certificate of Insurance (COI)
- Business License or Articles of Incorporation
- Signed Vendor Code of Conduct

Additional requirements apply under certain conditions.

IT/Software vendors must also provide:

- Completed Security Questionnaire

Vendors that access customer information, confidential business information,
or company systems must complete the Security Questionnaire regardless of
vendor category.

Missing required documentation pauses the onboarding workflow until the documents are received.
