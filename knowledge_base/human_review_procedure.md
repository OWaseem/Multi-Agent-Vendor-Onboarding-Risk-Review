# Human Review Procedure

Human review is required whenever automated processing cannot confidently
approve or reject a vendor.

Examples include:

- High-risk vendors
- Foreign vendors
- Policy exceptions
- Sanctions matches
- Reviewer disagreement
- Maximum revision attempts reached

The approval request should include:

- Vendor information
- Risk score
- Reviewer recommendation
- Supporting policy references

Human reviewers may approve, reject, or request additional information.

The final decision is recorded in the vendor management system.
