# Policy Exception Guidelines

Certain onboarding requests may qualify for a policy exception.

Examples include:

- Missing documentation with approved temporary waiver
- Urgent operational need
- Executive business justification

Policy exceptions should only be recommended when the requester provides
sufficient written justification.

The reviewer must document:

- Exception reason
- Business impact
- Risk assessment
- Recommended expiration date

All policy exceptions require human approval.

Automatic approval is not permitted for exception requests.
