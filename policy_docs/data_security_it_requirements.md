# Data Security & IT Vendor Requirements

## IT/Software Vendor Requirements

category: IT/Software

IT/Software vendors must complete a security questionnaire and undergo
enhanced due diligence. Because they touch software and internal systems,
every IT/Software vendor is routed to the human-approval gate for sign-off
even when the risk score is low. A missing security questionnaire in this
category is a high-risk flag.

## Data-Sensitive Vendor Requirements

category: general

Any vendor that will handle company or customer data is data-sensitive and
must complete a security questionnaire. Data-sensitive vendors are always
routed to the human-approval gate. A new vendor with no prior relationship
that is data-sensitive carries an additional risk flag.

## Certifications

category: general

Certifications on file, such as SOC 2 or ISO 27001, are recorded in the vendor
master record and should be cited by the planner as supporting evidence, but
they do not override the security questionnaire requirement.

## Security Questionnaire Standard

category: general

The security questionnaire documents access controls, encryption practices,
data handling and retention, and incident response procedures. It is required
regardless of certifications on file.
