# Sanctions & Watchlist Handling Policy

## Watchlist Screening

category: general

Every vendor is screened against the organization's sanctions and watchlist
before onboarding. A match is treated as the highest-severity risk flag and
escalates the case to human review on its own, even when all documents are
complete.

## Matches Are Never Auto-Approved

category: general

A vendor appearing on the watchlist must never be auto-approved for standard
onboarding. The case is escalated to the human-approval gate, which makes the
final decision. The risk/compliance reviewer documents the match and its
reason in the verdict.

## Verification

category: general

The watchlist check matches vendor names case-insensitively against the mock
watchlist table. Partial name matches are treated as hits and recorded, and
the requester is not expected to resolve watchlist matches themselves.

## Precedent

category: general

Past vendors found on the watchlist have consistently been escalated, never
auto-onboarded. See the precedent write-ups for representative outcomes.
