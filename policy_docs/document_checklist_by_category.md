# Required Document Checklist by Vendor Category

## General Checklist — All Vendors

category: general

Every vendor, regardless of category, must submit: a W-9 or Tax ID form, a
certificate of insurance (COI), a business license or articles of
incorporation, and a signed vendor code of conduct. A request missing any of
these four is incomplete and is paused for resubmission.

## Banking / ACH Form

category: general

The banking / ACH payment form is required before a vendor may reach final
"approved" status. It is intentionally NOT a blocker for the onboarding
review itself: the review may proceed without it, and the form is collected
ahead of the final approval write.

## IT/Software Vendors

category: IT/Software

In addition to the general checklist, IT/Software vendors must submit a
completed security questionnaire. A missing security questionnaire for an
IT/Software vendor is a high-risk flag and must be documented in the risk
review.

## Data-Sensitive Vendors

category: general

Any vendor flagged data-sensitive must submit a completed security
questionnaire in addition to the general checklist. This applies regardless
of vendor category.

## Professional Services

category: Professional Services

Professional services vendors must satisfy the general checklist. No
additional documents beyond the general set are required for this category.

## Logistics

category: Logistics

Logistics vendors must satisfy the general checklist. No additional documents
beyond the general set are required for this category.

## Facilities

category: Facilities

Facilities vendors must satisfy the general checklist. No additional documents
beyond the general set are required for this category.

## Marketing

category: Marketing

Marketing vendors must satisfy the general checklist. No additional documents
beyond the general set are required for this category.

## Office Supplies

category: Office Supplies

Office supplies vendors must satisfy the general checklist. No additional
documents beyond the general set are required for this category.
