# Vendor Onboarding Policy

## Purpose

This policy governs how the organization evaluates and onboards new vendors.
Every vendor request is processed through a three-stage workflow: intake and
document completeness, onboarding planning, and risk/compliance review. The
workflow is grounded in this policy and in the required document checklist
published separately.

## Document Completeness

A request is considered complete only when every required document for the
vendor's category has been submitted. The base set of documents is required
for all vendors: W-9 or Tax ID form, certificate of insurance, business
license or articles of incorporation, and a signed vendor code of conduct.
IT/Software vendors and any vendor that will touch company or customer data
must also complete a security questionnaire.

## Onboarding Outcomes

A request resolves to one of four outcomes: standard onboarding, which
auto-completes once the reviewer approves; paused awaiting requester
information when documents are missing; escalated to human review for
high-risk vendors, policy exceptions, or repeated failed revisions; or
rejected when the human approver declines the onboarding.

## Policy Exceptions

A policy exception may be recommended when a vendor fails a standard
criterion but the requester has provided a credible business justification.
Exceptions never take effect without human sign-off; the human approver makes
the final call.

## Revision Limit

The risk/compliance reviewer may send a draft recommendation back to the
planner for revision. Revisions are capped at two. A third revision request
automatically escalates the case to human review.

## Mandatory Human Review

Enhanced due diligence requires human sign-off for every IT/Software vendor
and for any vendor flagged data-sensitive, regardless of the calculated risk
score. These cases are always routed to the human-approval gate.
