# Precedent Vendor Risk Write-Ups

## Standard Onboarding Precedent

category: general

A US-based office supplies vendor with a prior relationship and a complete
document set was scored zero on risk and approved for standard onboarding.
The workflow completed automatically without human review. No flags were
raised.

## Enhanced Review Precedent — IT Vendor

category: IT/Software

A new IT/Software vendor with a complete document set including the security
questionnaire was scored below the escalation threshold on flags alone.
Because the category mandates enhanced due diligence, the case was routed to
the human-approval gate, which approved the onboarding.

## Revision Precedent — Foreign Entity

category: general

An existing facilities vendor based in Germany submitted a complete document
set. The planner over-flagged the foreign entity and recommended high risk.
The reviewer corrected the recommendation to standard onboarding, because a
lone foreign-entity flag scores below the escalation threshold, and approved
the case.

## Escalation Precedent — Data-Sensitive Foreign Vendor

category: general

A new, data-sensitive vendor based in Germany with no security questionnaire
accumulated foreign-entity, missing-questionnaire, and new-vendor flags plus
inherent data risk, far above the escalation threshold. The case was escalated
to the human-approval gate.

## Watchlist Precedent

category: general

A vendor that matched a sanctions watchlist entry was escalated immediately.
The human approver rejected the onboarding, and the vendor's final status was
recorded as rejected.
